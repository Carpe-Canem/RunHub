Local Legend Watch

Before a run: which Local Legend titles are about to slip and which are within reach. After a run: how your mileage compares with your normal.

Your keys and data stay in this browser. This page has no server. It talks to Strava directly from your browser, and nothing is sent to the site owner or anyone else. Disconnect wipes everything.

Connect your Strava (about 3 minutes, once per browser)

Each runner uses their own free Strava API app. That's what keeps everyone's keys and data separate.

  1. Open strava.com/settings/api on a computer and log in.
  2. Fill in the form:
    Application Name
    Anything, such as “My Legend Watch”
    Category
    Visualizer
    Website
    Authorization Callback Domain

    Just the domain, no https:// and no path.
  3. Agree to Strava's terms, click Create, then upload any small image as the app icon.
  4. On the “My API Application” page, copy your Client ID and Client Secret (click “show”) and paste them below.
  5. On Strava's approval screen, leave both boxes ticked, including View data about your private activities, so your mileage is complete. Private runs never count toward Local Legends here.
